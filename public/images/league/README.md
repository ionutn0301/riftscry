# League references and artwork

- `summoners-rift.webp`: Riot Games’ Summoner’s Rift environment screenshot, resized and encoded as WebP. Original: https://cmsassets.rgpub.io/sanity/images/dsfx7636/news_live/7b747c9a6331b74a7518c5ea0d8d5e9d81440579-1920x1506.jpg . Published in https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/unleashing-the-elements/ .
- The home diorama in `src/lib/home-world.ts` is original procedural geometry inspired by the blue Nexus, turrets, wards, jungle, Infernal Drake, and Baron Nashor. No game models or client files are extracted. Environment reference: https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/how-to-play/ .
- Existing champion portraits and splash art use Riot Data Dragon via `assetFor` in `src/lib/investigation.ts`; https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol#data-dragon .

League of Legends and its characters and environments belong to Riot Games. RiftScry is an independent companion; the site’s existing Riot disclaimer applies.
