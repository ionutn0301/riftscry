# Official League environment artwork

`iceborn-vaults.webp` is Riot Games’ **Vaults of the Iceborn beneath Frosthorn Peak**, from the Freljord region gallery in Universe of League of Legends. It is used as the site’s painted atmosphere and the home camera’s continuous environment.

- Region page: https://universe.leagueoflegends.com/en_US/region/freljord/
- Official gallery data: https://universe-meeps.leagueoflegends.com/v1/en_us/factions/freljord/index.json
- Original artwork: https://cmsassets.rgpub.io/sanity/images/dsfx7636/universe_live/884a56e9c5937f91fdde2dfe53eeb4b0eeafddc5-1920x967.jpg?accountingTag=LoL
- Encoded as WebP (quality 88) at its original 1920 × 967 size. No painted details, characters or scenery were added or replaced.

The camera crops the intact illustration for each viewport; it does not extract or approximate Riot’s game models. The previous procedural Nexus, trees and monsters have been removed.

Champion portraits and splash art use Riot Data Dragon via `assetFor` in `src/lib/investigation.ts`; https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol#data-dragon .
