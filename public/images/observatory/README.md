# RiftScry — the observatory

Original atmospheric artwork generated for the cinematic RiftScry experience on
29 October 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool. This is an independent
science-fantasy environment, not Riot champion artwork or a depiction of an
official League of Legends location. The site’s existing user-provided brand
marks and official champion illustrations were not included or modified.

## Production assets

| File | Dimensions | Size | Treatment |

| `rift-horizon-mobile.webp` | 780 × 1040 | 72,052 bytes | Portrait crop focused on the right-side fracture; WebP quality 78. |

The generated original was 1672 × 941. Sharp performed format conversion,
resizing, and the explicit mobile crop only; it did not synthesize or repaint
image content. The interactive light and particles are separate code-rendered
layers. Useful information and actions remain HTML, independent of this artwork.

## Generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original cinematic background artwork
> for a high-end interactive League of Legends companion called RiftScry, not a
> UI mockup. Create one ultra-wide 16:9 panoramic anamorphic science-fantasy film
> still from the year 2077. A colossal oblique fractured dark-metal elliptical
> halo is suspended above a vast obsidian mountain valley and a dark ocean of
> cloud. The impossible halo occupies the RIGHT THIRD of the frame, towering at
> a monumental scale, subtly tilted in perspective. Several broken
> metallic/stone segments hover just apart, with a hairline icy-cyan energy seam
> along the inner circumference, a very restrained lavender spectral bloom,
> and tiny suspended stone fragments. The inside of the halo opens into a pale
> distant dimension with a narrow white horizon. Rich tactile weathered black
> rock, haze in deep layers, black water far below, a trace of warm ivory light
> on the remote horizon. Lighting is physically believable, moody, precise,
> extraordinary feature-film VFX quality, subtle film grain, excellent
> volumetric atmosphere, true depth and quiet majesty. Palette: near-black
> indigo #070b13, platinum/silver-white, muted icy cyan and a whisper of violet,
> never saturated neon. Composition: landscape wide 16:9; LEFT 40 PERCENT must
> remain very dark, low-detail, mostly empty atmosphere with minimal silhouetted
> terrain, because a website headline will be placed there later. Keep primary
> bright light and detailed rift toward x=72%, y=45%. Premium editorial framing,
> extraordinary scale. No characters, people, creatures, spacecraft, buildings,
> logos, letters, text, UI, borders, watermarks. Do not imitate any existing game
> artwork. Avoid a symmetrical centered portal, generic neon cyberpunk, busy
> cityscapes, ornate medieval gates, flat illustration, and overly saturated
> colors.
